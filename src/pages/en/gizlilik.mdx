# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 9 October 2026

At CineQ we care about our users' privacy and data security.

## 1. Data collected and local storage

The app does not store users' personal data, payment details or contact information on external servers. Your ticket selections, cart and theme preference are kept only in your device's local storage (`localStorage`).

## 2. KVKK and data protection

In line with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law no. 6698 (KVKK), no personal data is transferred to an external database. When the app data is cleared, all session records are deleted.

## 3. Cookies and permissions

No third-party tracking cookies are used inside the Tauri WebView.
